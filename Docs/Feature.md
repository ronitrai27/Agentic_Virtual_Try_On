🏆 Option 1: Live Voice "Smart Mirror" (Hands-Free Try-On)
The Problem: When standing back in front of a camera trying on clothes, touching the keyboard/mouse breaks the immersion.
The Demo: You stand back, look at the camera, and simply talk:
"Try on a navy denim jacket."
"Now find me a casual linen shirt under 1500."
"Save this look to my wardrobe."

Why Judges Love It: It transforms your laptop into a futuristic Retail Smart Mirror. No typing needed during live video try-on.
